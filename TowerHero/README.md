# Tower Hero 🏰

Build the ultimate tower defense! Strategically place towers to defend against waves of enemies in this action-packed strategy game.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Defend your base by strategically placing towers to stop enemy waves from reaching the end of the path!

### Controls
- **Click**: Select and place towers
- **Drag**: Move towers (if allowed)
- **Upgrade**: Click on towers to upgrade them
- **Back Button**: Return to main menu

### Game Flow
1. **Wave Start**: Enemies begin moving along the path
2. **Tower Placement**: Click to place defensive towers
3. **Strategic Defense**: Position towers for maximum coverage
4. **Upgrades**: Improve towers between waves
5. **Survive**: Prevent enemies from reaching your base!

## 🎯 Tower Types

### Basic Towers
- **Cannon Tower**: High damage, slow rate of fire
- **Machine Gun**: Fast firing, moderate damage
- **Missile Tower**: Area damage, good against groups
- **Laser Tower**: Continuous beam, high accuracy

### Special Abilities
- **Slow Towers**: Reduce enemy movement speed
- **Freeze Towers**: Temporarily stop enemies
- **Poison Towers**: Damage over time effects
- **Electric Towers**: Chain lightning between enemies

## 💡 Strategy Guide

### Placement Strategy
- **Chokepoints**: Place towers where enemies bunch up
- **Coverage**: Ensure no gaps in your defense
- **Range Optimization**: Maximize tower coverage areas
- **Path Control**: Force enemies through kill zones

### Economic Management
- **Resource Balance**: Manage money for towers and upgrades
- **Investment Priority**: Upgrade key towers first
- **Wave Preparation**: Save resources for difficult waves
- **Efficiency**: Get maximum value from each tower

### Advanced Tactics
- **Tower Synergy**: Combine different tower types effectively
- **Upgrade Timing**: Know when to upgrade vs. build new
- **Enemy Adaptation**: Adjust strategy for different enemy types
- **Emergency Response**: Handle breakthrough situations

## 🏆 Enemy Types

### Basic Enemies
- **Infantry**: Standard health and speed
- **Fast Units**: Low health, high speed
- **Heavy Units**: High health, slow movement
- **Flying Units**: Bypass some tower types

### Special Enemies
- **Armored**: Resistant to certain damage types
- **Regenerating**: Heal over time
- **Splitting**: Divide into smaller units when destroyed
- **Boss Units**: Extremely high health and special abilities
