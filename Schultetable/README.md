# Schulte Table 🎯

Train your focus, attention, and reading speed with this classic cognitive exercise! Find numbers in sequence as quickly as possible to improve your mental agility.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Click numbers in ascending order (1, 2, 3, 4, 5...) as quickly and accurately as possible!

### Controls
- **Click**: Select the next number in sequence
- **Timer**: Race against the clock for best times
- **Back Button**: Return to main menu

### Game Flow
1. **Start**: Click "Start Game" to begin
2. **Find Numbers**: Locate and click numbers in order (1, 2, 3...)
3. **Speed Focus**: Work as quickly as possible while staying accurate
4. **Complete**: Finish all numbers to see your time
5. **Improve**: Try again to beat your best time!

## 💡 Training Strategy

### Beginner Approach
- **Systematic Scanning**: Use consistent left-to-right, top-to-bottom pattern
- **Don't Rush**: Accuracy is more important than speed initially
- **Practice Regularly**: Short, frequent sessions are most effective
- **Stay Relaxed**: Tension slows down performance

### Advanced Techniques
- **Peripheral Vision**: Use wider field of view to spot numbers
- **Chunking**: Group numbers mentally to reduce search time
- **Rhythm Development**: Find your optimal clicking rhythm
- **Pattern Memory**: Remember common number placement patterns

### Performance Tips
- **Warm Up**: Start with easier grids before challenging ones
- **Break Time**: Take breaks to prevent mental fatigue
- **Track Progress**: Monitor improvement over time
- **Stay Consistent**: Regular practice yields best results
