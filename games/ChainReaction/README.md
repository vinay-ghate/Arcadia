# Chain Reaction ⚛️

A strategic grid-based game where timing and placement create explosive chain reactions! Plan your moves carefully to trigger massive cascading effects.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Place atoms on the grid to create chain reactions that eliminate your opponent's atoms while expanding your own territory!

### Controls
- **Click**: Place an atom on an empty cell or add to your existing atoms
- **Back Button**: Return to main menu

### Game Rules
1. **Placement**: Click on empty cells or your own atoms to add more
2. **Critical Mass**: Each cell has a maximum capacity based on its position
3. **Explosion**: When a cell reaches critical mass, it explodes!
4. **Chain Reaction**: Explosions can trigger neighboring cells to explode
5. **Capture**: Explosions convert opponent atoms to your color
6. **Victory**: Eliminate all opponent atoms to win!

## 🎯 Critical Mass System

### Cell Capacities
- **Corner Cells**: Maximum 1 atom (2 neighbors)
- **Edge Cells**: Maximum 2 atoms (3 neighbors)  
- **Center Cells**: Maximum 3 atoms (4 neighbors)

### Explosion Mechanics
- **Overflow**: When capacity is exceeded, cell explodes
- **Distribution**: Atoms spread to adjacent cells
- **Conversion**: All affected atoms become your color
- **Chain Effect**: Can trigger multiple explosions

## 💡 Strategy Guide

### Basic Strategy
- **Corner Control**: Corners explode easily - use strategically
- **Edge Building**: Build along edges for controlled explosions
- **Defensive Play**: Block opponent's potential chain reactions
- **Timing**: Wait for the right moment to trigger big chains

### Advanced Tactics
- **Chain Setup**: Create multiple cells near critical mass
- **Sacrifice Plays**: Give up atoms to prevent opponent chains
- **Territory Control**: Dominate sections of the grid
- **Endgame Planning**: Set up winning chain reactions

### Common Patterns
- **Corner Trap**: Force opponent into corner explosions
- **Edge Cascade**: Create rolling explosions along edges
- **Center Bomb**: Build up center cells for maximum impact
- **Defensive Wall**: Use atoms to block opponent expansion
