# Snake 🐍

The classic arcade game that started it all! Guide your snake to eat food and grow longer while avoiding walls and your own tail.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Control the snake to eat food and grow as long as possible without hitting walls or yourself!

### Controls
- **Arrow Keys**: Change snake direction (Up, Down, Left, Right)
- **WASD Keys**: Alternative movement controls
- **Touch/Swipe**: Swipe in desired direction on mobile
- **Back Button**: Return to main menu

### Game Rules
1. **Start**: Snake begins as a small segment moving forward
2. **Eat Food**: Guide snake to red food pellets
3. **Grow**: Each food eaten makes snake one segment longer
4. **Avoid**: Don't hit walls or your own tail
5. **Score**: Earn points for each food consumed
6. **Game Over**: Collision ends the game

## 🎯 Scoring & Progression

### Scoring System
- **Food Points**: Each food pellet increases your score
- **Length Bonus**: Longer snake = higher score multiplier
- **Speed Bonus**: Faster speeds give bonus points
- **High Score**: Best score saved locally

### Difficulty Progression
- **Speed Increase**: Snake moves faster as you grow
- **Tighter Spaces**: Longer snake makes navigation harder
- **Precision Required**: Advanced play demands exact timing

## 💡 Tips & Strategy

### Beginner Tips
- **Plan Ahead**: Think about where you're going, not just where you are
- **Use Edges**: Follow walls to create predictable patterns
- **Avoid Center**: Don't trap yourself in the middle of the screen
- **Stay Calm**: Panic leads to mistakes

### Advanced Strategy
- **Spiral Patterns**: Create systematic movement patterns
- **Wall Hugging**: Use walls as guides for safe movement
- **Tail Following**: Follow your own tail to create safe paths
- **Corner Management**: Master tight corner navigation

### Pro Techniques
- **Coiling**: Create tight coils to maximize space usage
- **Predictive Movement**: Anticipate where food will appear
- **Risk Assessment**: Know when to take chances vs. play safe
- **Pattern Breaking**: Vary your movement to avoid predictability
