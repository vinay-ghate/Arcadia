# Tic-Tac-Toe vs CPU 🎯

Challenge yourself against an intelligent AI opponent in this classic strategy game! Choose your difficulty and test your tactical skills.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Get three of your marks (X or O) in a row, column, or diagonal before the computer does!

### Controls
- **Click**: Place your mark on an empty cell
- **Keyboard**: Use Tab to navigate and Enter/Space to place marks
- **Back Button**: Return to main menu

### Game Setup
1. **Choose Your Mark**: Pick X or O (X always goes first)
2. **Select Difficulty**: Easy, Medium, or Hard
3. **Start Playing**: Click "New Game (vs CPU)"

## 🎯 Difficulty Levels

### Easy Mode
- **AI Strategy**: Basic blocking with occasional mistakes
- **Perfect For**: Learning the game and casual play
- **Win Rate**: High chance of winning with good strategy

### Medium Mode
- **AI Strategy**: Smart blocking and center control
- **Perfect For**: Regular players wanting a fair challenge
- **Win Rate**: Balanced gameplay with strategic thinking required

### Hard Mode
- **AI Strategy**: Advanced tactics with fork creation and blocking
- **Perfect For**: Experienced players seeking maximum challenge
- **Win Rate**: Very difficult to win, draws are common

## 💡 Tips & Strategy

### Basic Strategy
- **Control the Center**: The middle square (position 5) is most valuable
- **Block Opponent**: Always block the computer's winning moves
- **Create Forks**: Try to create two winning opportunities at once

### Advanced Tactics
- **Corner Strategy**: Start with corners for better winning chances
- **Opposite Corners**: If opponent takes a corner, take the opposite
- **Edge Trap**: Use edges strategically to create winning combinations

## 🏆 Scoring System

- **Win**: +1 point for you
- **Loss**: +1 point for CPU
- **Draw**: +1 point for ties
- **Running Total**: Track your performance across multiple rounds
