# 2048 🔢

The addictive number puzzle game! Slide tiles to combine numbers and reach the elusive 2048 tile. Simple to learn, challenging to master!

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Combine tiles with the same number to create larger numbers. Reach the 2048 tile to win!

### Controls
- **Arrow Keys**: Move tiles up, down, left, or right
- **WASD Keys**: Alternative movement controls
- **Touch/Swipe**: Swipe in any direction on mobile devices
- **Back Button**: Return to main menu

### Game Mechanics
1. **Start**: Begin with two random tiles (2 or 4)
2. **Move**: Use arrow keys to slide all tiles in one direction
3. **Combine**: Tiles with the same number merge into one
4. **New Tile**: After each move, a new tile (2 or 4) appears
5. **Win**: Reach the 2048 tile to achieve victory!
6. **Lose**: Game ends when no moves are possible

## 🎯 Scoring System

### Points Calculation
- **Tile Merging**: Score equals the value of the new tile created
- **Example**: Combining two 64s creates a 128 tile and adds 128 points
- **Running Total**: Your score accumulates throughout the game
- **High Score**: Best score is saved locally

### Scoring Strategy
- **Plan Ahead**: Think several moves in advance
- **Corner Strategy**: Keep your highest tile in a corner
- **Build Systematically**: Create ascending number patterns

## 💡 Tips & Strategy

### Beginner Tips
- **Choose a Corner**: Pick one corner and keep your highest tile there
- **Build in Rows**: Create organized rows of ascending numbers
- **Don't Rush**: Take time to plan your moves
- **Avoid Random Moves**: Every move should have a purpose

### Advanced Strategy
- **Snake Pattern**: Build numbers in a snake-like pattern
- **Edge Control**: Keep high-value tiles along edges
- **Merge Planning**: Set up multiple merges in sequence
- **Space Management**: Always leave room for new tiles

### Common Mistakes
- **Corner Abandonment**: Moving your highest tile from its corner
- **Scattered Building**: Spreading high tiles across the board
- **Impatient Moves**: Making moves without considering consequences
- **Ignoring Small Tiles**: Not managing 2s and 4s effectively
