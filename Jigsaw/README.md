# Jigsaw Puzzle 🧩

Relax and challenge your mind with beautiful jigsaw puzzles! Drag and drop pieces to recreate stunning images in this classic puzzle experience.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Arrange scattered puzzle pieces to recreate the complete image!

### Controls
- **Drag & Drop**: Click and drag pieces to move them around
- **Snap to Place**: Pieces automatically snap when placed correctly
- **Zoom**: Use mouse wheel or pinch to zoom in/out (if available)
- **Back Button**: Return to main menu

### Game Flow
1. **Choose Puzzle**: Select from available images
2. **Scattered Pieces**: Pieces start randomly positioned
3. **Sort & Organize**: Group pieces by color, edges, or patterns
4. **Build Gradually**: Start with edges or distinctive areas
5. **Complete**: Place the final piece to finish!

## 🎯 Puzzle Strategy

### Getting Started
- **Edge First**: Find and connect all border pieces
- **Color Sorting**: Group pieces by dominant colors
- **Pattern Recognition**: Look for distinctive shapes or details
- **Corner Priority**: Corners are easiest to identify and place

### Advanced Techniques
- **Section Building**: Complete small sections before connecting
- **Shape Analysis**: Study piece shapes and connection points
- **Reference Image**: Use the complete image as a guide
- **Systematic Approach**: Work methodically rather than randomly

### Organization Tips
- **Workspace Management**: Keep pieces organized in groups
- **Edge Collection**: Separate all flat-edged pieces first
- **Color Families**: Group similar colored pieces together
- **Progress Tracking**: Build from completed sections outward

## 🏆 Difficulty Levels

### Piece Count Variations
- **Easy (25-50 pieces)**: Perfect for beginners and quick games
- **Medium (100-200 pieces)**: Balanced challenge for most players
- **Hard (300-500 pieces)**: Serious puzzle solving experience
- **Expert (1000+ pieces)**: Ultimate challenge for puzzle masters

### Complexity Factors
- **Image Detail**: More detailed images are harder
- **Color Variety**: Monochrome images increase difficulty
- **Pattern Repetition**: Repetitive patterns make placement tricky
- **Piece Shape**: Irregular shapes add complexity
