# Tetris 🧩

The legendary puzzle game that defined a generation! Arrange falling blocks to create complete lines and achieve the perfect clear.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Arrange falling tetrominoes (7-piece shapes) to create complete horizontal lines that disappear, preventing the stack from reaching the top!

### Controls
- **Arrow Keys**: Move and rotate pieces
  - **Left/Right**: Move piece horizontally
  - **Down**: Soft drop (faster falling)
  - **Up**: Rotate piece clockwise
- **Spacebar**: Hard drop (instant placement)
- **Back Button**: Return to main menu

### Game Mechanics
1. **Falling Pieces**: Tetrominoes fall from the top
2. **Line Clearing**: Complete horizontal lines disappear
3. **Scoring**: Points for placed pieces and cleared lines
4. **Speed Up**: Game gets faster as you progress
5. **Game Over**: Stack reaches the top of the playing field

## 🎯 Scoring System

### Points Breakdown
- **Single Line**: 100 points × level
- **Double Lines**: 300 points × level
- **Triple Lines**: 500 points × level
- **Tetris (4 lines)**: 800 points × level
- **Soft Drop**: 1 point per cell
- **Hard Drop**: 2 points per cell

### Level Progression
- **Lines Cleared**: Every 10 lines increases level
- **Speed Increase**: Higher levels = faster falling pieces
- **Score Multiplier**: Higher levels give more points

## 💡 Strategy Guide

### Basic Techniques
- **Flat Stacking**: Keep the surface as flat as possible
- **Tetris Setup**: Save space for 4-line clears with I-pieces
- **T-Spin Basics**: Learn to rotate T-pieces into tight spaces
- **Hold Function**: Save pieces for later use

### Advanced Strategies
- **T-Spin Doubles**: Advanced technique for high scoring
- **Perfect Clear**: Clear the entire board for bonus points
- **Combo Chains**: Chain line clears for multiplied points
- **Finesse**: Optimal key sequences for piece placement

### Piece Management
- **I-Piece**: Save for Tetris clears when possible
- **T-Piece**: Most versatile, learn T-spin setups
- **S/Z Pieces**: Tricky shapes, plan placement carefully
- **O-Piece**: Simple square, good for filling gaps
- **L/J Pieces**: Useful for creating overhangs

## 🧩 The Seven Tetrominoes

### I-Piece (Cyan)
- **Shape**: Straight line (4 blocks)
- **Best For**: Tetris clears (4 lines)
- **Strategy**: Save for maximum impact

### O-Piece (Yellow)
- **Shape**: 2×2 square
- **Best For**: Filling gaps and flat building
- **Strategy**: Simple placement, no rotation needed

### T-Piece (Purple)
- **Shape**: T-shaped (3+1 blocks)
- **Best For**: T-spins and versatile placement
- **Strategy**: Most complex, learn rotation patterns

### S-Piece (Green)
- **Shape**: S-shaped zigzag
- **Best For**: Creating overhangs
- **Strategy**: Plan ahead, can create difficult situations

### Z-Piece (Red)
- **Shape**: Z-shaped zigzag
- **Best For**: Mirror of S-piece usage
- **Strategy**: Similar to S-piece, requires planning

### J-Piece (Blue)
- **Shape**: L-shaped (left-facing)
- **Best For**: Creating and filling overhangs
- **Strategy**: Versatile piece, good for various situations

### L-Piece (Orange)
- **Shape**: L-shaped (right-facing)
- **Best For**: Mirror of J-piece usage
- **Strategy**: Complement to J-piece patterns
