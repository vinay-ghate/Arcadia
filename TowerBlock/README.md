# Tower Block 🏗️

A 3D block stacking game where precision and timing are key to building the tallest tower possible!

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objective
Stack blocks as high as you can by perfectly aligning them. The higher your tower, the higher your score!

### Controls
- **Click** or **Spacebar**: Place the moving block
- **Back Button**: Return to main menu

### Gameplay
1. **Start**: Click "Start Game" to begin
2. **Watch**: A block will move back and forth across the screen
3. **Time**: Click or press spacebar when the block is perfectly aligned with the block below
4. **Stack**: Successfully placed blocks become part of your tower
5. **Precision**: Misaligned blocks get chopped off, making the next block smaller
6. **Challenge**: Keep stacking until you miss completely!

## 🎯 Scoring

- **+1 point** for each successfully placed block
- **Perfect alignment** gives you a bonus and maintains block size
- **Misaligned blocks** reduce the size for the next block
- **Complete miss** ends the game

## 💡 Tips & Strategy

### For Beginners
- **Take your time** - there's no rush to place blocks
- **Watch the pattern** - blocks move in predictable patterns
- **Aim for the center** - perfect alignment is rewarded

### Advanced Techniques
- **Perfect Placement**: Try to align blocks perfectly to maintain full size
- **Rhythm**: Find the rhythm of the moving blocks
- **Focus**: Concentrate on the edge alignment rather than the whole block
- **Patience**: Don't panic as the tower gets higher

## 🏆 Achievements to Aim For

- **First Tower**: Stack 5 blocks
- **Steady Builder**: Stack 10 blocks
- **Architect**: Stack 15 blocks
- **Skyscraper**: Stack 20 blocks
- **Master Builder**: Stack 25+ blocks
